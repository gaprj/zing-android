2. Accounts and developer-operated servers

Zing does not require a Zing account. The app does not provide a developer-operated account or cloud-sync service for pantry records. Most information you enter is stored and used locally on your device. The optional online functions below send only the information needed for the request you initiate.

3. Information stored locally on your device

Depending on which features you use, Zing can store:

  • Pantry and products: product names, categories, quantities, units, lots, storage locations, minimum-stock settings, expiry dates and product details you choose to save.
  • Recipes and shopping: shopping-list items, recipes you create or save, ingredient comparisons, recipe-related notes and ratings supported by the app.
  • Preferences and reminders: language, theme, date and measurement settings, notification preferences, reminder schedules, timers and other app settings.
  • Optional nutrition profile: values you choose to enter, which can include age or date of birth, sex used for an estimate, height, weight, training frequency and a nutrition goal. These details are used by on-device informational estimates and suggestions, not for medical advice.

The developer does not receive these local records through an account or synchronization server. The current Android configuration disables automatic app-data backup. If you choose to export a JSON backup, you choose where to save it; the exported file is outside the developer's control and must be managed or deleted separately.

Information stored only on your device remains there until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. The developer cannot remotely delete data that has never been transmitted to a developer-operated server.

4. Camera and barcode scanning

The camera permission is used to scan barcodes. Camera frames are processed on the device using CameraX and Google's ML Kit Barcode Scanning SDK. Zing does not intentionally upload the camera stream for barcode recognition. Google components may process technical usage or diagnostic data according to their own documentation and the behavior of the installed components. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

If you use online product lookup, the decoded barcode is sent in an HTTPS request to product-catalogue services. Zing may query Open Food Facts and, as a fallback, Open Products Facts. Those providers may receive the barcode you searched for and ordinary connection information such as IP address, request time and request headers. Zing's request User-Agent includes the developer contact email. The lookup request does not intentionally include the full camera stream, nutrition profile or full pantry inventory.

Returned product information may be incomplete or inaccurate. See the Open Food Facts terms of use and the relevant Open Products Facts service information.

5. Online recipe discovery

When you start online recipe discovery, Zing sends search terms and lexical variants based on up to four pantry ingredients to the Italian Wikibooks MediaWiki API. The nutrition profile and complete pantry inventory are not intentionally sent as a whole. Wikibooks receives the query and ordinary connection information such as IP address, request time and request headers. Ingredient matching is performed locally, and the app links to the original Wikibooks page for source context.

Wikibooks and Wikimedia process requests under their own terms and privacy notices. See Wikibooks copyright and reuse information and Wikimedia's privacy policy.

6. Optional banner advertising and consent

The optional support banner is disabled by default. It is requested only after you enable “Pubblicità volontaria (Opt-in)” in Zing's settings and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) indicates that an ad request is permitted. If you disable the setting, Zing removes the banner and does not request new banner ads while the option remains disabled.

Zing uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request and display the banner, and Google UMP to refresh advertising consent status and display or reopen privacy-choice forms when required. These services may process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app/device information, ad request and interaction data, approximate location derived from IP, and diagnostics needed to provide, protect, measure or troubleshoot advertising. The precise processing depends on the user's privacy choices, region, ad configuration and Google's service behavior. The SDK providers and their participating vendors may receive information needed to deliver and measure the ad.

The in-app opt-in setting controls whether Zing displays and requests the support banner; it is separate from the privacy choices collected through UMP. Users can manage the advertising privacy choices from the in-app privacy options entry point when UMP requires it. Personalized advertising is not requested unless the applicable consent/privacy state allows it. For details about Google processing, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's consent-management requirements.

7. Google Play purchases — Zing Pro

Zing's code contains Google Play Billing support for a one-time Pro product identified as zing_pro_lifetime. Availability depends on the product being configured and enabled in Google Play Console. When an in-app purchase is available and used, Google Play handles checkout and payment details. The app receives purchase status and product-related information needed to unlock the entitlement; Zing does not directly receive a user's full payment-card details. Google's terms and privacy notices apply to the Play transaction.

8. Intended audience and nutrition information

Zing is a general household pantry, recipe and food-planning app intended primarily for adults and families managing food and shopping. It is not designed or marketed specifically to children under 13. This does not mean it is technically impossible for a child to access the app.

Nutrition estimates and suggestions are informational only. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition and are not a substitute for professional advice. The nutrition profile is optional; you can leave its fields blank and delete values from the app.
